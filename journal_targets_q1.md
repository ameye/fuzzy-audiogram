## Recommendation
1. **First choice if fee is a hard constraint: Ear and Hearing via the traditional (non-OA) route — $0 APC.** Same journal, same prestige, same topical fit; just don't opt into gold OA. Only choose OA if a funder mandates it.
2. **If gold OA is required:** Trends in Hearing (≤ $2,650, computational audiology fit) or **CEORL (≤ $1,000, cheapest Q1 OA)** — but confirm CEORL's computational-methods appetite and waiver eligibility first.
3. **If framing as AI methodology:** Artificial Intelligence in Medicine (or IEEE J-BHI) — $0 traditional route, maximizes AI-reader reach while keeping clinical validation central.
4. Avoid leading with IEEE TFS/Fuzzy Sets unless the fuzzy-system contribution is elevated (novel membership/rule design), not just application.
